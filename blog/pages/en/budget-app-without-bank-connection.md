A budget app without bank connection keeps one job for you — typing in what you spend — and does the adding up. Bill Tracker is built that way on purpose: it never asks for your banking login, and there's no account to create before you can start.

## Why choose a budget app without bank connection

Apps that read your bank account save you the typing, and for plenty of people that's a fair trade. Others would rather keep their budget separate from their bank, for reasons like these:

- **You don't want to hand over banking access.** A manual budget needs nothing from your bank: no login, no consent screen to renew, no connection that breaks.
- **Your money isn't in one place.** Cash, two cards, a partner's account, a meal voucher — when you type the amount yourself, it doesn't matter how you paid.
- **Typing makes you notice.** Entering 7.50 for a sandwich takes a few seconds, and it's exactly those seconds that keep you aware of where the month is going.

## How a manual budget works day to day

In Bill Tracker you sort your spending into **envelopes** — groceries, housing, transport, fun — and give each one a monthly amount. You can also set just one total for the month, or no budget at all and simply track.

- **Logging in seconds:** amount, envelope, save, on a large numpad. Logging works offline, so it also works in a shop with no signal.
- **Siri:** say "Log expense in Bill Tracker" and the expense is logged without opening the app.
- **Recurring income and bills:** salary, rent, phone contract and subscriptions can repeat every month automatically, so you only type what changes. Any expense you've already logged can be set to repeat monthly.
- **A month that starts on payday:** if you're paid on the 25th, your budget month can run from the 25th to the 24th.
- **Income with sources:** salary, freelance work, gifts or your own labels.

A quick example. Sam gives the Groceries envelope 400 for the month. In the first week Sam logs 62.40 for the big shop, 18.90 at the market and 7.50 for a sandwich. The envelope shows 88.80 spent and 311.20 left — without a single bank transaction being read.

## What stays on your phone

There's no account to create — no email, no password. There are no ads, and your data isn't sold.

A personal budget stays on your device. If you share a space with a partner or your household, only that shared space is synced, on servers in the EU (Frankfurt), and only its members can see it. You can export every transaction in a space as a CSV file, and you can delete all your data in one step.

## What Bill Tracker doesn't do

Being a manual app means some things are simply not there, and it's better to know before you install:

- It doesn't import bank transactions, read SMS or scan receipts. Every expense is typed.
- It doesn't send bill due-date reminders.
- It doesn't calculate who owes whom. A shared space is one common budget, not a bill splitter.

If you want a budget you fill in yourself and understand at a glance, that short list is the point.

## FAQ

### Is there a budget app that doesn't link to your bank account?

Yes. Bill Tracker never connects to a bank or card. You type each expense yourself, choose an envelope and the app shows what's spent and what's left.

### Does the app work offline?

Logging works offline: amount, envelope, save. A personal budget stays on your device. Shared spaces are synced so that every member sees the same numbers.

### Do I need an account or an email address?

No. There's no account to create — no email and no password. You install the app and start budgeting.

### Isn't typing every expense slow?

An entry is three steps on a large numpad: amount, envelope, save. Fixed costs like rent or subscriptions can repeat monthly on their own, and Siri can log an expense without opening the app.

### Is Bill Tracker free?

The free plan covers 1 space for up to 2 people with up to 10 envelopes, insights, recurring bills and CSV export. Premium adds unlimited spaces, people and envelopes and expenses in other currencies.

### Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Bill Tracker runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later). An Android version is on its way — you can join the waitlist on the [home page](/#download).
