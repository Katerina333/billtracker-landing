A shared expense tracker app has one job in a shared flat: make the money you spend together visible to everyone who lives there. Bill Tracker does that with a shared space — one budget for the household, envelopes for the things you buy together, and insights that show who spent what — so nobody has to keep the tally in their head or chase receipts at the end of the month.

## Why shared household money gets messy

Living with flatmates is mostly about the small purchases. Rent is usually clear; it's everything around it that causes friction:

- **Everyone buys a bit.** One of you picks up washing-up liquid, another does the big shop, a third pays for the pizza on Friday. Each purchase is small and nobody remembers them all.
- **The bills land on one person.** The internet or electricity account is often in one name, so one flatmate quietly pays more than the others for months.
- **Group chats are not a ledger.** "I'll send you my share" gets lost between memes. By the time someone asks, nobody is sure what was paid.
- **Nobody wants to be the accountant.** Keeping a spreadsheet for the whole house is a thankless job, and it stops the week that person is busy.

What helps is a budget that lives on the phones everyone already carries, where each person logs their own purchases at the till.

## How Bill Tracker works as a shared expense tracker

- **One space for the household.** A space has its own members, envelopes, budget and currency. Create one called "Flat on Elm Street" and invite everyone who lives there with a link.
- **Envelopes for what you share.** Start from ready-made categories or make your own — Shared groceries, Cleaning & household, Internet, Electricity & gas, Takeaway nights — with custom names, icons and colours. Give each envelope a budget, set one total for the month, or track without a budget at all.
- **Everyone logs, everyone sees.** Amount, envelope, save, on a large numpad. Every member logs what they buy, and everyone sees the same numbers. Logging works offline, so a basement supermarket with no signal is not a problem.
- **Monthly bills log themselves.** Set the internet or energy bill as a recurring expense and it's logged automatically every month.
- **A month that starts when you want.** The budget month can start on any day — on rent day, for example — instead of the 1st.
- **Insights per person.** Top spending categories, change since the previous month and who spent what, filtered by person or by envelope, for any period.
- **No bank connection, no account.** Bill Tracker never reads anyone's bank account, and there's no email or password to create.

## A shared budget, not a bill-splitting calculator

Bill Tracker shows everything the household spent and who paid for it. It does **not** calculate who owes whom, and it doesn't settle debts between people. It's a shared budget, not a bill splitter.

For most shared homes that's enough. You agree on what counts as shared, everyone logs their purchases, and at the end of the month you look at the totals per person and settle up by hand — equally, or in whatever proportion you've agreed. If you prefer to do the sums in a spreadsheet, CSV export gives you every transaction in the space. Our guide on [splitting costs with different incomes](/blog/budgeting-for-couples-with-different-incomes/) walks through proportional shares by hand; the same maths works for flatmates.

## Your personal money stays yours

Flatmates share a fridge, not their whole finances. Only the shared space is synced — on servers in the EU (Frankfurt) and visible only to its members. A personal budget stays on your own device. Your own spending only shows up in the household's numbers if you log it in the shared space.

## FAQ

### Is there a free shared expense tracker app?

Bill Tracker's free plan gives you 1 space with up to 2 people and up to 10 envelopes, including insights, recurring bills and CSV export. A space for three or more flatmates needs Premium; joining someone else's space by invite is always free.

### Does Bill Tracker calculate who owes whom?

No. It shows who spent what in a shared space, filtered by person or envelope, but it doesn't calculate debts or settle them. Most households use the totals per person and settle up by hand.

### Can my flatmates see my personal spending?

Only what you log in the shared space. A personal budget stays on your device, and a shared space is visible only to its members.

### Do we have to connect a bank account?

No. Bill Tracker never connects to a bank or card. Everyone types their own expenses, and there's no account to create.

### Can we track monthly bills like internet and electricity?

Yes. Set a bill as a recurring expense and it's logged automatically every month, in the envelope you choose.

### Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Bill Tracker runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later). An Android version is on its way — you can join the waitlist on the [home page](/#download).
