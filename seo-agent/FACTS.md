# Product facts — the only claims the SEO agent may make about the app

If something is not on this list, don't claim it. When unsure, describe the general technique
(how to budget, how to split costs) rather than an app feature.

Checked against app **1.1.0** (App Store, 2026-09-29). Update this file with every app release.

## The app
- Name: **Bill Tracker** (App Store name "Bill Tracker: Budget Planner"; site: billtrackerx.com).
  Publisher: ITPM Group.
- **iPhone and iPad only, iOS 15 or later.** There is **no Android app, no web app, no Mac or
  desktop app and no Apple Watch app.** Never say or hint otherwise.
- App languages: English, Ukrainian, Hungarian, Norwegian, German, French, Portuguese, Spanish.
- App Store link: use `{{app-cta}}` in posts; in FAQ answers link to `#download`. Never invent URLs.

## What it is
- A **manual envelope-budgeting app for households**: you give each category ("envelope") a monthly
  budget and log what you spend; the app shows what is spent and what is left.
- **No bank connection, ever.** The user types every expense. Never say it imports bank
  transactions, reads SMS, scans receipts or syncs with any bank or card.
- **No account to create** — no email, no password. No ads. Data is not sold.

## Budgeting
- Envelopes with custom names, icons and colours; ready-made categories to start from.
- A budget per envelope, a total monthly budget, or no budget at all.
- The budget month can start on any day (for example payday), not only the 1st.
- Envelopes can be reordered by press-hold-drag.
- Income tracking with sources (salary, freelance, gifts, custom).
- **Recurring** income and expenses are logged automatically every month; any already logged
  expense can be set to repeat monthly. (Monthly only — don't claim weekly or yearly repeats.)
- **Not in the app:** rolling leftover money into next month, savings goals, debt payoff plans,
  bill due-date reminders or notifications, investment tracking, receipt photos, home-screen widgets.
  Explain how to do these by hand if a post needs them; never say the app does them.

## Logging
- Amount → envelope → save, on a large numpad. Works offline.
- Siri: "Log expense in Bill Tracker" logs an expense without opening the app.
- An expense can be logged in a different currency than the space's (Premium). It is **kept as
  typed, with no conversion**, and listed separately from the budget totals. Never say the app
  converts currencies or fetches exchange rates.

## Sharing
- A **space** (workspace) has its own members, envelopes, budget and currency: home, a trip, a
  renovation. Switch between spaces in one tap.
- Invite people with a link; everyone logs and everyone sees the same numbers.
- People without a phone (kids, grandparents) can be added by name.
- Insights show who spent what; filter by person or by envelope.
- The app does **not** calculate who owes whom or settle debts between people. It is a shared
  budget, not a bill-splitting calculator.

## Insights and data
- Income vs expenses, top spending categories, change since the previous month, for any period.
- Full history; CSV export of every transaction in a space; delete all data in one step.
- A personal budget stays on the device. Only shared spaces are synced, on servers in the EU
  (Frankfurt), visible only to their members.
- Light and dark mode.

## Free vs Premium
- **Free:** 1 space, up to 2 people in it, up to 10 envelopes, insights, recurring bills, CSV
  export. Joining someone else's space by invite is always free.
- **Premium:** unlimited spaces, people and envelopes; expenses in other currencies. Sold as a
  monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. A free trial exists.
- **Never state prices, trial lengths or discounts** — they differ by country and change.

## Never
- Financial, tax, legal or investment advice. Describe budgeting methods; don't tell readers what
  to buy, where to invest or how to file taxes. No promises ("save €500 a month").
- Competitor names in a negative light, fake statistics, invented testimonials, ratings or awards.
- Statistics without a source you can name; if you can't, leave the number out.
